Chapters

The problem

Booking a technician is still word of mouth

Hard to find trusted help

Households ask friends and call several people before finding someone available.

No price or time certainty

Prices are negotiated by phone and arrival times are guesswork.

Technicians lack a channel

Skilled freelancers and small companies have no steady way to get jobs, manage staff, or build a reputation.

The solution

One platform, three products

Customer app

Find a service, choose a company or top-rated technician, agree on price and time, track live, pay and rate.

Technician app

Receive jobs in real time, accept or propose a price or time, run the job, manage employees and promotions.

Admin dashboard

Approve technicians, set service prices, monitor jobs, settle payments and see reports.

How it works
